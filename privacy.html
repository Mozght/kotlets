Privacy policy

This site is a set of static calculator pages. The numbers you type stay in your browser. The only optional tracking is Google Analytics 4, and only if you accept cookies.

What stays in your browser

The trip budget, group split, ski trip and vacation savings calculators run entirely in your browser. There is no account and no server-side form. The figures you enter are not sent to us. If you refresh or close the page, they are gone.

Google Analytics and cookies

If you choose Accept on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to see which pages are visited, whether a calculator is used, and whether a visitor follows a link to our planners on Etsy. That uses a cookie (and similar storage) set by Google. If you choose Decline, we do not use analytics cookies. Advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation stay denied in either case: this site does not run ads and does not use GA4 for advertising.

Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage) so the banner does not appear again. You can change it at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. After you change it, the new choice applies to later visits from that browser.

Google may process analytics data on servers outside the country you are in, including the United States, under Google’s analytics terms. We do not sell your data, and we do not send calculator inputs to Google or to anyone else.

What we learn if you accept

Page views, approximate location and device information that GA4 collects by default, a calculator_used event the first time you change a calculator on a page, and an etsy_click event when you follow a link to etsy.com. That is enough to see which tools are useful. It is not enough to identify you as a customer, and it is not combined with a shop account.
